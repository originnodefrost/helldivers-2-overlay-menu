## 🧩 About

**Helldivers 2 Overlay** is a curated list of Helldivers 2 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, hitbox viewer, mission timer
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loadout catalog, stratagem manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — mission layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Mission Timer — track mission elapsed time and objectives
- Hitbox Viewer — visualize enemy and friendly hitboxes for study
- Replay Notes — record and review mission runs
- Playback Speed — adjust recorded playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Loadout Catalog — browse weapons, armor, and stratagems
- Custom Skin Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Marker Visibility — improved objective markers

### Aimbot
- Tactical Map Notes — study mission layouts and spawn points
- Stratagem Cooldown — monitor cooldowns for educational timing
- Jump Pack Notes — chain maneuvers in training
